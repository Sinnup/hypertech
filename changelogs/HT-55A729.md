## Summary
Part 3: the /viz frontend renders the expected steps up front in gray and fills
them in color as they progress, with a live substep line — for pipelines and
commands alike.

## Changes
- **static/viz.html**:
  - Steps strip + substep line (CSS + DOM): `renderSteps`, `setStepState`
    (running/done/error → blue/green/red), `setSubstep` (current detail).
  - SSE: handle `plan_ready` (render gray strip; hide DAG for kind=command),
    `agent_progress` (substep), `step_update` (command stages); augment
    agent_start/end/error to also color the strip + show substeps; agent_end
    "stopped" shows red.
  - Lobby auto-switches to a command's ticket on `plan_ready`.

## Verification

- Ingest→SSE: POSTing `plan_ready`/`agent_progress` to /viz/ingest delivers them
