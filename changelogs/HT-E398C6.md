## Summary
`/new --scenario poc|internal|production <prompt>` parsed the flag for its reply
but never forwarded it to the pipeline, so the orchestrator re-classified the
prompt. An Android TPV prompt sent with `--scenario production` therefore ran as
`poc`. The explicit scenario is now honored end to end.

## Changes
- **core/state/pipeline_state.py**: new `scenario_override` channel;
  `new_state(..., scenario=None)` records it (and seeds `scenario`).
- **main.py**: `run_pipeline(..., scenario=None)` forwards it into `new_state`.
- **agents/orchestrator/agent.py**: when `scenario_override` is set, skip
  `classify()` and use the forced scenario (conf 0.95) — drives `_build_plan`
  to the right plan. (Also fixed a pre-existing typo `relse`→`resume` in main.py.)
- **core/notifications/slack_commands.py**: `/new` passes `scenario_hint` to
  `run_pipeline`.
- **tests/e2e/test_scenario_forwarding.py** (new): override recorded in state;
  orchestrator honors production/internal and does NOT call `classify`.
