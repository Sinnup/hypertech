# HT-F0032C — /reload-kb streams progress and reports results

- **Scenario**: fix
- **Agents**: (Slack /reload-kb + Drive ingestion)
- **Branch**: feature/HT-F0032C
- **Created**: 2026-06-26 04:05 UTC
- **Status**: completed

## Summary
`/reload-kb` posted "reload started" and then nothing — no progress, and if the
ingestion hung (e.g. a wedged ChromaDB upsert) it went silent forever. It also
posted the final notice to the agent-status channel, and a "0 files / errors"
result was still reported as success.

## Changes
- **ingestion/google_drive/ingest.py**: `run(folder_id=None, progress=None)` —
  optional `callable(str)` invoked at each stage (downloading, downloaded N,
  loaded N, embedding/upserting).
- **core/notifications/slack_commands.py** `_handle_reload_kb`: posts a "started"
  alert, streams stage progress, and reports a clear result to #pipeline-alerts —
  ✅ with ingested/skipped/error counts, ⚠️ when nothing was ingested (with the
  reason), or ❌ on exception.

## Verification
`uv run pytest tests/e2e/test_reload_kb_progress.py` → 2 passed (progress fires
at each stage; safe without a callback).
